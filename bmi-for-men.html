BMI for Men

Men are held to the same adult BMI ranges as women, but body composition differs on average, so the same number can mean different things.

The same ranges apply

A man with a BMI of 18.5–24.9 is in the healthy weight range, the same as anyone else. There is no separate male chart.

Muscle can inflate BMI

Men carry more muscle on average, and people who lift weights or play contact sports often register as overweight despite low body fat. If you are very muscular, BMI may overstate your risk.

Where fat is stored matters

Men tend to store fat around the abdomen. This visceral fat is linked with heart disease and diabetes even when BMI looks acceptable. A waist measurement of 94 cm (37 in) or more is commonly flagged for increased risk, and 102 cm (40 in) or more for high risk.

Practical checks to pair with BMI

  • Waist circumference at the navel level
  • Blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Weekly activity, aiming for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise

Frequently asked questions

Is a BMI of 25 bad for a man?

Not necessarily. It is the start of the overweight band, and context such as muscle mass and waist size decides whether it is a concern.
