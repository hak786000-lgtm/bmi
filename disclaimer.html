Medical Disclaimer

Please read this notice before relying on any information on this website.

General information only

Everything on this website, including the BMI calculator, is provided for education. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

BMI is a screening tool

BMI cannot assess your overall health. Results can be misleading for children, pregnant women, older adults, athletes and others, as described in BMI limitations. Children must be assessed with growth-chart percentiles; see BMI for children.

Talk to a professional

Always consult a qualified health professional about your weight, diet, exercise or symptoms. Never delay or ignore medical advice because of something you read here. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

No guarantees

We make reasonable efforts to keep content accurate and up to date, but we cannot guarantee it is complete or error-free.
