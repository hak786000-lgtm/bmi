Adult BMI Calculator

This page is for anyone aged 20 and over. Use the calculator, then read how age and other factors shape what your result means.

Calculate your BMI

For adults aged 20 and over. This tool does not apply to children or teenagers — see BMI for children.

Adult ranges

  • Below 18.5: underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: healthy weight
  • 25.0 to 29.9: overweight
  • 30.0 and above: obesity

Age changes the picture

As people get older they tend to lose muscle and height while gaining fat, so BMI can look reassuring even when body composition has worsened. For adults over 65, some research suggests that a BMI slightly above 25 is not harmful, while being underweight carries clear risks. A clinician can help set an appropriate target.

Other factors a doctor will consider

Family history, smoking, blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, sleep and activity level all affect health risk. See BMI limitations for the full list of what the number misses, or the healthy weight guide for next steps.

Frequently asked questions

What age is BMI for?

The adult categories are intended for people 20 and over. For ages 2 to 19, percentiles are used instead.
