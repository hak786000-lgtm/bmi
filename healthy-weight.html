Healthy Weight Guide

A healthy weight is a range, not a single figure, and it depends on more than your BMI. Here is how to find yours and what to do with it.

Find your range

Use the BMI calculator to see the weights that correspond to a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 at your height, or look it up on the BMI chart. Treat the whole range as acceptable rather than aiming for its lowest point.

Measure more than weight

Waist circumference, blood pressure, blood glucose and cholesterol give a better view of health than the scale. If you are outside the healthy BMI range, these are good questions for a doctor.

Habits that help

  • Build meals around vegetables, legumes, whole grains and lean protein
  • Move most days; walking counts
  • Sleep seven to nine hours when possible
  • Aim for gradual change, for example 0.25–0.5 kg (0.5–1 lb) a week if losing weight

When to seek help

See a clinician if you have lost weight without trying, have a history of eating disorders, or are planning a major change in diet. Crash diets are rarely sustainable and can harm health. Read the limitations of BMI to keep the number in perspective.

Frequently asked questions

How fast is it safe to lose weight?

Many guidelines suggest about half to one kilogram a week at most, but individual advice should come from a clinician.
