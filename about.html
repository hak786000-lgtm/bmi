About This Website

This site exists to make a common health number easier to calculate and, more importantly, easier to understand.

Our purpose

BMI is widely used and widely misunderstood. We provide a fast calculator and plain-language guides so you can see what your result means and what it does not.

How content is written

Pages follow publicly available guidance from bodies such as the World Health Organization and the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. We aim to explain limits clearly and avoid exaggerated claims. This site is not staffed by medical professionals, and nothing here replaces personal medical advice.

What we do not do

We do not sell products, show fake reviews, or collect your measurements. If we add advertising or tracking in future, the privacy policy will say so.

Get in touch

Spotted an error or have a suggestion? Visit our contact page. To start using the tools, go to the BMI calculator.
