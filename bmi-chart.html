How to read the chart

Locate your height, then see which column your weight falls into. A weight between two columns’ limits is simply the nearest band; the exact BMI is easier to get from the BMI calculator. The meaning of each column is explained on the BMI categories page.

Who this chart is for

These ranges apply to adults. They should not be used for anyone under 20, where growth patterns change what is typical; see BMI for children.

Frequently asked questions

Is the BMI chart the same for men and women?

Yes. The adult cut-offs are the same for both sexes, although body composition differs; see the pages on men and women for context.
